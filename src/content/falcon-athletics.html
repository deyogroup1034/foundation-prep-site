FPA Athletics
Student Athletics are a vital resource for the training of young minds and hearts. These athletic programs provide opportunities outside the classroom for students to practice and experience biblical principles taught at home and in the school.

FPA students may participate in any of the various extracurricular activities and clubs available for his/her age group, as long as the student meets all requirements for participation, is in good academic standing, and the family is in good financial standing with the school. Any fees associated with the student activity (athletic fees, materials fees, uniform fees) must be paid.

Homeschool students may also participate in FPA athletics. As FPA students must be accepted to Foundation Preparatory Academy through a formal admissions process, homeschool students who wish to participate in FPA athletics will also participate in an abbreviated application process involving, prayer, completing and application form and a family interview.

Students wishing to participate in athletic clubs and activities must sign and agree to the Athletic Policy Handbook. All student athletes must also have a current sport’s physical and be cleared by their doctor for participation.
 
Basketball

 
Open to: 5th through 12th-grade boys and girls
Season: Winter
Description: Basketball is a team sport suited for developing personal fitness, specific athletic skills, as well as kingdom character on and off the court. Coaches work with students and parents to help each player reach his potential. The basketball teams practice together but may compete at different events. Parents will be required to provide transportation for practices and/or games. Students are required to have a sports physical. Participation is dependent upon good academic and financial standing as per school policies. No experience is necessary, and no tryout is required. Final teams will be determined based on the number of athletes available to play.
 
Girls Volleyball

 
Open to: 5th through 12th-grade girls (5th grade practice only)
Season: Fall, Spring conditioning
Description: Girls’ volleyball will be a competitive team sport with required practices and games. Parents will be required to provide transportation for practices and/or games. Students are required to have a sports physical. Participation is dependent upon good academic and financial standing as per school policies. All skill levels are welcome and it is our mission to “train students and athletes to use their gifts to glorify God”. No experience is necessary, and no tryout is required.
 
Tennis

 
Open to: 5th through 12th-grade girls and boys
Season: Spring
Description: Tennis is an individual sport with required practices and matches. Parents will be required to provide transportation for practices and matches. Students are required to have a sports physical. Participation is dependent upon good academic and financial standing as per school policies. All skill levels are welcome, no experience is necessary, and no tryout is required.
