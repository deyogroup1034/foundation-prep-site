The University-Model ® is a five-day program. Students spend 2-3 days in the on-campus classroom and 2-3 days in their “satellite classroom” at home. Both classrooms are under the supervision of a professional educator.

At Foundation Preparatory, Kindergarten – 2nd grade is a full-time program while 3rd through 12th graders may choose from a full-time program or supplement a homeschool curriculum by choosing a part-time schedule with a la carte classes.
