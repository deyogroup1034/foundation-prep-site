Ministry Board

The Head and supreme authority of this ministry is the Lord Jesus Christ Himself (Eph. 1:22; Col. 1:18). His will, which will be discerned through the prayerful and appropriate application of His written Word (cf. 2 Tim. 2:15), must be followed in all matters pertaining to this ministry. The overseers of the ministry–that is, those who have been given responsibility for its government–will be the members of the governing Ministry Board. The authority of the governing Ministry Board members is invested in the board as a corporate whole rather than in the members individually.
